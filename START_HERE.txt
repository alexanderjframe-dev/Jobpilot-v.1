JOBPILOT — FIRST LAUNCH

1) Unzip the folder.
2) Windows: double-click run.bat
   Mac or Linux: open Terminal here and enter ./run.sh
3) Open http://127.0.0.1:8765 in your web browser.
4) Fill in Applicant profile (name, email, resume).
5) Adjust all 27 sliders under Preferences and save.
6) Click Find jobs. The default feed is REMOTE ONLY; for LOCAL jobs,
   connect employers' Greenhouse/Lever job board tokens or add job URLs.
7) Save and approve a job before clicking Apply assist.

AUTOMATIC FORM FILL (optional):
  Windows: .venv\Scripts\activate.bat
  Mac/Linux: . .venv/bin/activate
  Then: python -m playwright install chromium

NO BACKGROUND WORK IN CHAT: The scheduler runs only while this local
application is active on your computer. It doesn't submit to arbitrary
job sites and it won't bypass human verification steps.
